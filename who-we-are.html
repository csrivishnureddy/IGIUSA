Our Culture

“Talent is the multiplier. The more energy and attention you invest in it, the greater the yield” - Marcus Buckingham

Listening to understand our people while celebrating their unique qualities, and differences are at the core of IGI's core values. IGI takes pride in honoring and respecting all our employees' unique differences, be it race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability and more. Bringing our best to the workplace means being who we are – we believe this makes us better individually, collectively and ultimately drives creativity, innovation and success.

We believe that when determining the right people, the good-to-great companies place greater weight on character attributes than on specific educational background, practical skills, specialized knowledge, or work experience. We strive to be a business with a trusting culture, which give employees flexibility in their roles and empower them to make good decisions because we trust in the skills, knowledge, and work ethic they’ve hired for.

To have a successful business, we believe in surrounding ourselves with people of diverse perspectives and this has proven to be successful model as our employees work hard to reach the company’s goals and avoid breaking the organization’s trust in them.
