CATI Services

CATI (Computer-Assisted Telephone Interviewing) is a method used in market research where interviewers conduct surveys over the phone using a computer system to guide the interview process. CATI studies provide an effective way of understanding consumers’ views on every element of your strategy – from the customer experience to your communications strategy.

At IGI, we have a dedicated team to provide CATI services. We will help you with your CATI requirements with different aspects such as Sample Management, CATI System Setup, Interviewer Training and Management, Data Security and Compliance etc.

IGI has a large database of the North American marketplace for Computer-Assisted Telephone Interviewing (CATI) services, we offer a streamlined and efficient approach to collecting high-quality data through telephone surveys.
