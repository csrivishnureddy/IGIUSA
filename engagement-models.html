Staff Augmentation & Contracting Services

Pay for the actual hours and the resources for the project.

  • Flexibility: We offer flexibility in terms of project scope and requirements. Clients can adjust project goals and features as they go, which benefits projects with evolving or unclear requirements.
  • Hourly Rates: We have a defined rate card for each skill and resource involved in the project.
  • Transparency: Clients can see detailed reports of hours worked by each resource and the expenses incurred, allowing for better tracking and budget management.
  • Risk Sharing: Unlike fixed-price contracts where the risk of scope changes or delays falls primarily on the service provider, we distribute these risks more evenly.
  • Communication and Collaboration: Regular updates and discussions help manage expectations and ensure alignment on project goals.

  • Proactive Maintenance: IGI takes a proactive approach to IT management, ensuring systems are updated, monitored, and optimized to prevent issues before they occur.

Hybrid Onshore offshore

This is a strategic approach to optimize resource allocation, cost-efficiency, and operational effectiveness. This model blends the advantages of onshore, domestic and offshore, international resources to leverage the strengths of both.

Distributing tasks between onshore and offshore teams can mitigate risks related to talent shortages, market fluctuations, geopolitical instability, and regulatory changes.

Benefits:

  • Cost Efficiency
  • Access to Specialized Skills
  • 24/7 Productivity
  • Enhanced Competitiveness
  • Local Presence and Customer Satisfaction

Campus and Next Generation Hybrid Model

The Next Generation Hybrid Model combines the best of both on-campus and remote work environments, offering flexibility and scalability. This approach allows IT service companies to tap into a global talent pool, enabling employees to work from various locations while maintaining a connection to central offices for periodic collaboration and training.

Advanced digital tools and cloud-based platforms facilitate seamless communication, project management, and data security. The hybrid model supports a balanced work-life integration, reduces overhead costs, and enhances resilience to disruptions. By blending the stability of the Campus Model with the agility of remote work, IT service companies can optimize performance, innovation, and employee satisfaction.
